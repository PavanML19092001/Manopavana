KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our cold-pressed cooking oils and order fulfillment.

Refined oils undergo chemical bleaching and extreme heating over 200°C. MANOPAVANA oils are sourced directly from traditional wood-press artisans, settled naturally, and packed hygienically at our Bangalore facility with zero external heat or chemicals, preserving natural nutrients and authentic aroma.

Because our oils are completely unrefined and free from chemical filtration, we allow the oil to settle naturally through micro-mesh cotton cloths. The slight sediment at the bottom is pure seed fiber, a true mark of unrefined cold-pressed oils.

Our cold-pressed oils maintain peak freshness for 9 to 12 months from the packaging date. Store them in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and keep the bottle tightly sealed.
